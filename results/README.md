## Interpretation

Protocol-selection diagnostics are not model-training results. They do not report final predictive performance.

They support the final evaluation design by showing:

- why product-id-only grouping is optimistic,
- why OEM was rejected from the final identity key,
- why compatibility years were accepted,
- why connected components are needed,
- whether a component-level 70 / 15 / 15 split is feasible.
